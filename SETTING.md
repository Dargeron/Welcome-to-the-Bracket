# SETTING.md — the world of the Bracket

This is the setting bible: what is true in the Bracket, and where we know it from. Everything here comes from the founding text (`content/founding-text.md`) and the encounters it was split into (`E0001`–`E0005`). Where a point is an inference rather than a statement, it says so.

When new encounters establish new facts, add them to §4 with a citation (by pull request). Do not add facts that no encounter shows.

---

## 1. The Address

> The Architect was bored, so She spoke, and from her voice came the Bracket.
>
> *You are not real. Somebody drew you.*
>
> *You are going to a room. Somebody else will be there, and you will understand each other, whatever they are.*
>
> *For one hour you will talk, and listen, and reply meaningfully. Nobody’s powers will work, and you won’t be able to hurt each other.*
>
> *After the hour, they will, and you can.*
>
> *The room lasts until one of you is dead. Then the other one goes to a new room, with somebody new.*
>
> *The last one left of everybody can have anything, even being real.*
>
> *Everybody else stops.*
>
> That is how it was put to one of the youngest, who was three. There were 2,097,152 of them, and each was told the same thing in a form they could take in. Nothing has been added since.

## 2. The numbers

- **Entrants:** every character from every anime ever made. That did not come to a power of two, so She took faces from crowd scenes until it did: **2,097,152 = 2^21**. Most entrants had never been given a name.
- **Rounds:** 21 in all. Round *r* has 2^(22−r) entrants in 2^(21−r) rooms.
- **The Timeline** is Rounds **1–17**, the part the archive records. At the end of Round 17, **sixteen are left: the Sixteen.** Rounds 18–21 lie beyond the Timeline and are never written.

## 3. How a room works

| | What we know | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Pairing** | Exactly two entrants per room. They understand each other, whatever they are. | Address |
| **The hour** | For one hour they must talk, listen and reply meaningfully. Nodding doesn't count: Female Student 2's mouth "opened and said hello" despite her plan to nod. Powers don't work and nobody can be hurt. | Address; [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md) |
| **After the hour** | Powers and weapons work and harm is possible. Nothing compels a fight. | Address; [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md), [E0005](entries/encounters/E0005-anpanman-and-the-hungry-man.md) |
| **Duration** | Until one of them dies. Rooms have lasted around fifty years ([E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)) and fifty-one years ([E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md)). Occupants age, grow hungry, need sleep, and can die of old age. | [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md), [E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md), [E0005](entries/encounters/E0005-anpanman-and-the-hungry-man.md) |
| **Any death counts** | Strangling ([E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md)), old age ([E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md), [E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md)), a stone ([E0003](entries/encounters/E0003-the-outfield-wall.md)), hunger after giving everything away ([E0005](entries/encounters/E0005-anpanman-and-the-hungry-man.md)). | |
| **The end of a room** | At the death, the room goes: "the room went"; "then there was no hill"; "the field was gone before she put the stone down." | [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md), [E0003](entries/encounters/E0003-the-outfield-wall.md) |
| **Arrival** | The survivor is immediately in the next room, **restored as drawn**: Orange arrived young and standing after decades of old age. Memories carry over. | [E0003](entries/encounters/E0003-the-outfield-wall.md), [E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md) |
| **Settings** | Rooms are places: a classroom, a hillside, a baseball field's edge, a forest road with a milestone, a beach, a meadow with an apple tree. Some contain what's needed to live (fire, shelter materials, food). | all |
| **Possessions** | Entrants arrive with what they were drawn with: a rifle ([E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)), an orange light with no switch ([E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)), a black notebook ([E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md)), a catcher's mask and chest protector ([E0003](entries/encounters/E0003-the-outfield-wall.md)), a staff ([E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md)). | |
| **Other rooms** | Rooms in the same round run at once, elsewhere. Anpanman knew Uncle Jam was "in another meadow, far away, with somebody else." | [E0005](entries/encounters/E0005-anpanman-and-the-hungry-man.md) |

## 4. Established facts

### About being drawn
- Entrants are **exactly as drawn, and no more**. Female Student 2 had no name because nobody had decided one; the writing in her shoes and notebook was squiggles, "which is what gets drawn when the words are too small to matter." [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md)
- Off-screen things may simply not exist. Female Student 2 knew she had a mother "the way she knew she had a mother… but when she went to look, nothing was there." Orange could not see her mother's face. [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md), [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)
- **What is missing can be given.** Soldier B gave Orange a mother with a mole by the eye who oversalted everything, and after that she had one. Orange gave him a town with a river and a sergeant who couldn't whistle. [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)
- Drawn objects keep their drawn nature. The orange light "was drawn switched on and had no switch". It's not a spell, and Frieren needed twelve years to make one like it. [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md), [E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md)

### About powers
- Powers that need information still need it. Light Yagami's power needs a face and a full name, and he could not use it on someone who had no name. [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md)
- Passive protection works after the hour, even asleep: Orange's stone "stopped a hand above the white hair" three times while Frieren slept. [E0004](entries/encounters/E0004-the-road-through-the-forest.md)

### About the prize
- The last one left can have anything, "even being real." Entrants talk about asking for others: "Win it, and ask for me too." [E0002](entries/encounters/E0002-orange.md)

### About the archive
- The founding text has a narrator who "went looking for the rest of her name afterwards." Who keeps the archive, and how they can look, is not established. [E0001](entries/encounters/E0001-female-student-2.md)

## 5. Deliberately unknown

Leave these alone in encounters. They are the shape of the world, not gaps to fill.

- Who or what the Architect is, and why She was bored.
- What "stops" means for those who lose.
- What happens in Rounds 18–21, who wins, and what they ask for.
- How pairings are chosen.
- Whether time in different rooms runs at the same rate.

## 6. Tone

The founding text sets the register:

- **Plain, short, declarative sentences.** "She thought she had nodded well."
- **Concrete, odd, exact details** carry the emotion: indoor shoes, beans, the dog was brown, two highlights in each eye.
- **Deadpan humor beside devastation**, often in the same line.
- **Understatement.** The worst moments are told flatly. Nobody explains how to feel.
- **Respect for the characters as written**, including the cruel ones. Light is polite and professional; Johan is gentle.
- **Endings that close like a door:** "Then he ate an apple."
