# Welcome to the Bracket

The Architect was bored, so She spoke, and from her voice came the Bracket.

Every character from every anime ever made was entered, one bracket, single elimination. That did not come to a number that halves cleanly, so She took faces from the crowd scenes until it did. Most of the entrants had never been given a name.

This site is the archive of what happened in the rooms. It is written by its readers. Anyone can add a room, and anyone can vote on one. This page has the rules of the Bracket, which nobody can change, and the rules of the archive, which keep everybody's rooms consistent with each other.

If you'd rather start with a story, read [[E0001|Female Student 2]], or [[founding text|the founding text]] from the beginning.

## The Address

Each of the {{entrants}} entrants was told the same thing, in a form they could take in. This is how it was put to one of the youngest, who was three:

> *You are not real. Somebody drew you.*
>
> *You are going to a room. Somebody else will be there, and you will understand each other, whatever they are.*
>
> *For one hour you will talk, and listen, and reply meaningfully. Nobody's powers will work, and you won't be able to hurt each other.*
>
> *After the hour, they will, and you can.*
>
> *The room lasts until one of you is dead. Then the other one goes to a new room, with somebody new.*
>
> *The last one left of everybody can have anything, even being real.*
>
> *Everybody else stops.*

Nothing has been added since.

## The rules of the room

1. **Two to a room.** Every room holds exactly two entrants. They understand each other, whatever they are: a dog and a demon lord can talk.
2. **The hour.** For the first hour they must talk, listen, and reply meaningfully. Powers don't work and nobody can be hurt. Nodding [[E0001|does not count]].
3. **After the hour.** Powers return, weapons work, and harm is possible. Nothing forces a fight, and some rooms last [[E0002|fifty years]].
4. **The room lasts until one of them is dead.** It does not matter how: violence, old age, hunger, choice. Then the room goes.
5. **The survivor goes on** to a new room in the next round, with somebody new, and arrives as they were drawn. Memories come with them.
6. **Everybody else stops.**

At the end of Round {{finalRound}}, sixteen are left. They are **the Sixteen**. What happens to them in Rounds {{afterFinal}}–{{totalRounds}} is outside the Timeline and is never written.

## The Sixteen

Sixteen are left. Some of them are already known:

{{sixteen-roster}}

A character becomes one of the Sixteen in one of two ways:

- **A character entry designates them**, while places remain and the character hasn't stopped on record.
- **They win a Round {{finalRound}} room.** Whoever walks out of Round {{finalRound}} is one of the Sixteen.

One of the Sixteen can never stop in any encounter. Two of the Sixteen can never share a room.

## The rules of the archive

The archive has two kinds of entries.

**Encounters** are where the writing happens. An encounter is one room: two characters, one round, one death. Anyone may imagine any meeting between any two characters, as long as it fits the rules above and everything already in the archive. Every encounter fixes four facts that never change afterwards:

| Fact | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Character A** and **Character B** | the two entrants in the room |
| **Round** | 1 to {{finalRound}} |
| **Who stopped** | exactly one of the two |

**Character entries** come afterwards. A character entry tells, in the past tense, the path one character took through the Bracket, reconstructed from the encounters that have been published, like an encyclopedia article. It never adds events of its own. Each character page also lists their encounters, round by round, and which rounds are still open to writers.

From those four facts, the archive enforces:

1. **One room per round.** A character has at most one encounter in each round, so at most {{finalRound}} encounters in all.
2. **Death is final.** Once an encounter has a character stop in Round *n*, nobody may write that character into any round after *n*.
3. **No contradicting the record.** If a character is already on record alive in Round 9, no new encounter can kill them in Round 4. They won Round 4; you can still write how.
4. **First come, first canon.** Encounters are accepted in order of arrival. If two people write Frieren's Round 5, the first one accepted is Frieren's Round 5. When all of a character's possible rounds are written, that character is full.
5. **The Sixteen cannot stop.** Not in any round, by anyone's hand.
6. **Round {{finalRound}} makes the Sixteen.** Its survivor joins them, so once all sixteen places are filled, every Round {{finalRound}} room must be won by one of them. Round {{finalRound}} has exactly {{roomsFinal}} rooms.
7. **Rooms are finite.** Round *n* has a fixed number of rooms. Round 1 has {{roomsFirst}}, so this only matters near the end.

All of this is checked automatically when an encounter is submitted. A submission that breaks a rule is turned away with an explanation.

## Votes

At the end of every encounter there is a vote from 1 to 10. When an encounter receives its **{{quorum}}th vote**, the median of its first {{quorum}} votes decides its fate:

- **{{threshold}} or higher**: it stays. It is ratified, and later votes are recorded but change nothing.
- **Below {{threshold}}**: it is removed from canon and kept in the [[deprecated|deprecated archive]]. The rounds it used are open again and the death it described never happened.

There is no protection against voting twice. The archive trusts you.
