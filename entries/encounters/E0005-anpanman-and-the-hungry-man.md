*In one room [[E0004|the question]] was the whole of it. One of the two there had about four hundred words and meant every one. This is the room as he had it.*

---

Anpanman woke up in a meadow.

There was no bakery. There was no Uncle Jam and no Cheese. There was not even Baikinman. There was only a man sitting in the grass.

“Hello!” said Anpanman. “I am Anpanman!”

“Hello,” said the man. “My name is Johan.”

A big voice had told Anpanman that he was only a story. Anpanman did not mind. Stories are nice.

The voice had also said they must talk for one hour. So they talked.

“Tell me about yourself,” said Johan.

Anpanman told him about Uncle Jam, who bakes. He told him about Cheese, who barks. He told him about children who get lost in the woods and are hungry. Johan listened to all of it. He was a very good listener.

“But what about you?” said Johan.

Nobody had ever asked Anpanman that. Anpanman thought and thought.

“I am Anpanman,” he said. It was the only thing he could think of!

“And what about you?” asked Anpanman.

“I don’t know,” said Johan. He smiled when he said it.

Then the hour was over. Now Anpanman could fly again. He could punch again, too. But he did not want to punch Johan, and Johan did not want to punch him. So they sat in the meadow.

The meadow had an apple tree. Anpanman brought Johan an apple.

“Thank you,” said Johan. He put it down in the grass.

The next day, Anpanman brought him berries.

“Thank you,” said Johan. He put them down beside the apple.

On the fifth day Johan did not get up.

“Are you hungry?” asked Anpanman.

“Yes,” said Johan. “Very hungry.”

“Then eat your apple!”

Johan smiled at him. He did not eat his apple.

Anpanman looked at the apple. He looked at the berries. Anpanman is not silly. He knew why they were in the grass. He knew where Uncle Jam was, too. Uncle Jam was in another meadow, far away, with somebody else.

But here was a hungry man.

So Anpanman broke off a piece of his face. “Here,” he said. “Eat this.”

And Johan did.

“It’s very good,” said Johan. It was. Uncle Jam had made it.

The next day Johan was hungry again. So Anpanman broke off another piece.

And the next day.

And the next.

Most people only need a little piece. Johan needed all of it.

Soon Anpanman could not fly. That’s all right! Johan carried him into the shade. Johan was very gentle.

Soon Anpanman had one eye.

Then he had only a mouth.

“Are you still hungry?” asked the mouth.

“Yes,” said Johan.

Then Anpanman had no face at all. He could not see Johan. He could not say “You’re welcome.” He sat down in the shade to wait for Uncle Jam.

Johan waited with him.

It took a long time.

Then he ate an apple.
