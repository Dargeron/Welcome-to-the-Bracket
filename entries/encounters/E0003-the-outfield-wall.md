Then there was [[E0002|no hill]], and she had hold of the light, and she was standing up, which she hadn’t done without help in four years, at the edge of a baseball field.

She was young again. There was a boy in catcher’s gear, sixteen or so, third string on a team in a show about the pitcher. During the hour he asked whether they really had to, and he cried when she said they didn’t. She had a shelter up before dark. He said his grandmother was like that, good at things. He took off his mask and chest protector to sleep and set them side by side.

She had been choosing stones for sixty years. She went down to the outfield wall and chose one. She hadn’t been strong in a long time, and she used more than was needed. The field was gone before she put the stone down.
