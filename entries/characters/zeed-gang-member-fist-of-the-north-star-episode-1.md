The Zeed Gang Member was one of Zeed's raiders in the first episode of *Fist of the North Star*. He had a mohawk, spiked shoulder pads and a short knife, and was drawn laughing. He rode into a village on a motorbike shouting about water, kicked over a bucket, and was killed by Kenshiro; his last word on screen was "Huh?" He had no name.

## Round 1

No encounter has been recorded for his first room. In [[E0014]] he said that he had won it against a girl from the crowd at a school festival, using the knife at the end of the hour, and that afterwards he had found he "did not like being himself very much."

## Round 2: the valley

In [[E0014]] he arrived in a dry valley with a well and a stone hut and recognised [[Kenshiro]], who did not remember him. He did most of the talking during the hour. He said the three seconds after Kenshiro's strike in the episode were the part he thought about, because "it's the only bit I wasn't shouting." He asked whether it could take longer this time.

When the hour was over Kenshiro struck him and set his death for forty years, to the day. He made a mark on the hut wall for the first day. He carried water from the well in two buckets on a pole to grow beans and squash. His marks went round the hut and then onto rows of flat stones by the door. In about the twentieth year he could not stop counting them. He found out what he liked: squash more than beans, mornings, the sound of the bucket going down the well, and Kenshiro. He never got a name, and never asked for one.

On the last day he put on his shoulder pads and jacket and sat on the edge of the well. Around midday he felt the place under his ribs go warm and said, "Huh," quietly. This time, unlike the first, he kept his mouth shut. He stopped in Round 2.
