Orange was not listed in her source at all. She had been in the crowd at a concert, holding up an orange light that was drawn switched on and had no switch. The name was given to her in the Bracket by [[Soldier B]].

## Round 1: the hill

In [[E0002]] she and Soldier B failed to work his rifle, and then declined to use it. They lived together on the hillside for about fifty years. In that time each supplied the other's missing past. He gave her a mother, with a mole and a heavy hand with salt; she gave him a town with a river and a sergeant. When he died of old age, she was holding his hand. He had asked her to win and to ask for him too.

## Round 2: the outfield wall

She arrived in [[E0003]] young again and able to stand, at the edge of a baseball field, across from a [[Third-String Catcher]] of about sixteen. When he asked whether they really had to, she told him they didn't, and he cried with relief. She built a shelter before dark. After he had taken off his gear to sleep, she went down to the outfield wall, chose a stone, and killed him with it, using more force than was needed. This is the only recorded room in which she killed anyone.

## Round 3: the road

In [[E0004]] she met [[Frieren]] on a forest road. She tried to kill the elf in her sleep on the first night, and the stone stopped a hand above her head three times. By breakfast she understood that she could not win. She lived another fifty-one years, walking with Frieren, and told her about the mole, the salt, the river and the sergeant a little at a time. Every few years she told Frieren, "You could lose," and Frieren said no.

She died of old age in Round 3, with Frieren sitting by the bed. In the twelfth year of their walk, Frieren had made a copy of her orange light.
