Kiki is the young witch of *Kiki's Delivery Service*, who left home at thirteen with her black cat and ran a delivery service by broom from a bakery. Her cat, [[Jiji]], is a separate entrant in the Bracket.

## Rounds 1–10

No encounter has been recorded for Kiki's first ten rooms.

## Round 11: the cabin

In [[E0013]] she arrived at a cabin with her broom under her arm and noticed first that her cat was not there. The other occupant was [[Ursula]], a painter, who drew her during the hour. When Ursula called her the delivery girl, Kiki said, "I was the delivery girl," and that she did not know yet whether she still was. She explained that her flying had once gone away when she stopped believing she could do anything useful, and had come back after a delivery.

After the hour Ursula joked about hitting her with the stool. Kiki did not hit her, and did not fly out through the window or the roof. The two of them lived together in the cabin. Kiki learned the stove and learned to bake bread "that was only mostly bad." By the twentieth year she was mixing Ursula's paint for her; in the thirty-fourth she carried her between chair and bed. She had grown tall and strong in the room, and disliked it. She did not fly anywhere.

Near the end Ursula asked her to finish the painting of the sea, and Kiki said she could not paint. Ursula died at the end of the forty-first year while Kiki was reading to her. Kiki read to the end of the page before she looked up.

## Round 12 and after

The encounter ends with Kiki in her next room, a harbour. She set her broom against a bollard, sat down on the quay, and waited for the hour to start. That room has not been recorded.
