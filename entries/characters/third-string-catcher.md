The Third-String Catcher was a boy of about sixteen, third string on the team in a show about the pitcher. He is known only from [[E0003]].

## Round 2

He met [[Orange]] at the edge of a baseball field. During the hour he asked whether they really had to, and cried when she said they didn't. He told her his grandmother had been good at things, the way Orange was. He took off his mask and chest protector to sleep and set them side by side.

After he had settled down to sleep, Orange chose a stone from the outfield wall and killed him with it. He stopped in Round 2.
