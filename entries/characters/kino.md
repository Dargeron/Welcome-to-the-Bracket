Kino is the young traveller of *Kino's Journey*, who stays in any country for three days and no longer. In [[E0008]] she arrived without her motorrad. "He wasn't drawn onto me," she said. "He's his own person."

## Rounds 1–5

No encounter has been recorded for Kino's first five rooms.

## Round 6: the house in the field

In [[E0008]] she met [[Ginko]] in a small house in a field with a hedge around it. She counted one exit. During the hour she told him her rule: "Long enough to see it. Not long enough to start belonging to it."

After the hour she put a hand on the Persuader and looked at him. He said, "I'd rather you didn't, but I won't take it personally," and she took her hand off. It did not come up again.

On the evening of the third day she put on her coat, walked eleven minutes to the hedge, turned round, and came back and knocked. Ginko opened the door and asked how long she planned to stay. "Three days," she said. She did this every third evening for thirty-one years, more than three thousand times. Ginko set a fee for entry of one story, and she paid it with the countries she had seen.

In the nineteenth year, when he could no longer hold a cigarette, she lit one for him. When he told her he had broken his own rule in the first week, she said, "I know. I counted."

In the thirty-first year she came back from the hedge and nobody answered her knock. She found him in his chair, and stood in the doorway and said, in his voice, "Traveller. Welcome. How long do you plan to stay?" Then the room was gone.

## Round 7 and after

She arrived in a street with a fountain, her coat new, across from a man with a pigeon on his shoulder and a hat in his hand. She told him she would only be there three days, and when he said it would be longer, she said, "I know. I say it anyway." That room has not been recorded beyond its opening.
