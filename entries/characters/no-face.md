No-Face is a spirit from *Spirited Away*: tall and black, with a white mask and a painted mouth that does not move. In the film he came to the bathhouse, offered its staff gold, and swallowed some of them. In the Bracket he said little at first except "ah."

## Round 1

No encounter has been recorded for No-Face's first room.

## Round 2: the dock

In [[E0012]] his room was a dock beside flat grey water, with a lamp on a pole. His opponent was the [[Frog Worker (Spirited Away, the bathhouse)]], who recognised him from the bathhouse, where he had once been inside him. During the hour No-Face produced a lump of gold from an empty hand. Powers did not work, but he explained that the gold was "from before": he had been drawn holding it. The Frog took it and said thank you.

He did not fight after the hour. He went on making gold, and the Frog went on taking it, until there was a pile. No-Face asked what the thank you was for, and listened while the Frog explained *because* and *please*. In the third year he began to speak, in the Frog's own cracked, lisping voice, and afterwards spoke only in it. In the twentieth year the Frog asked to hear something of his own; the next morning No-Face answered, in the same voice, "I've been trying. This is what I have."

The Frog died of old age after fifty-three years, lying beside the pile, which came up to his hip. No-Face sat with him.

## Round 3 and after

The encounter ends with No-Face arriving in a third room, where a woman with her hair in a bun and her glasses pushed up on her head sat in a chair. He greeted her with the Frog's bathhouse welcome, "Is there anything the house can do for you?" When she said he sounded like a frog, he said, "It's what I've got." That room has not been recorded.
