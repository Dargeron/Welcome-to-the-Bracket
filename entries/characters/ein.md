Ein is the Pembroke Welsh corgi of *Cowboy Bebop*, a "data dog" taken from a research facility who lives aboard the Bebop with Spike, Jet, Faye and Ed. Asked whether his name was short for something, he said, "Einstein, probably. Nobody checked."

## Rounds 1–2

No encounter has been recorded for Ein's first two rooms.

## Round 3: the kitchen

In [[E0016]] he was on the linoleum floor of a small kitchen when [[Jiji]] arrived on the counter. Told he could talk, he answered, "No. You can hear." During the hour he described his crew: Spike walked into rooms as if he expected them to hit him; Jet put thought into a plant and forgot to water it; Ed might at any minute be on the ceiling; Faye lost money to people who were polite to her. People, he said, "listen. They don't hear. It's a different department."

He said somebody had been going to do something to him until somebody else took him out of the building, and that he knew a great deal, including the code to a bank in Ganymede, but had never been asked. If he were asked, he would say *woof*. When Jiji laughed, he said it had been a joke, and that he made about one a year.

After the hour he ate from the DOG bowl. He told Jiji that in here he could say what he liked, and "it turns out there's not much." He showed Jiji his trick: when Spike said "bang," he rolled over and lay with his paws in the air and his tongue out. They did not fight.

He was fourteen when he died. For his last month he did not get up for the bowl, and Jiji brought him water. On the last evening he asked whether Jiji wanted to see the trick, rolled over slowly, and put his paws in the air with his tongue out the side of his mouth. The room went while Jiji was still waiting for his ear to twitch. He stopped in Round 3.
