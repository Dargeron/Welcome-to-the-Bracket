Golgo 13 is the professional sniper of *Golgo 13*, who gives his name as Duke Togo. In [[E0011]] he wore a pale suit, carried a long case, and had very thick eyebrows; where he came from, the encounter says, he could get through a whole evening on three dots.

No encounter has been recorded for his first room.

## Round 2: the house with six cushions

In [[E0011]] he was standing against the alcove post when [[Sazae Fuguta]] found him. The hour made him reply. "Togo," he said. "Don't walk behind me." He explained that he hit anyone who came up behind him, without deciding to. He told her he shot people for a fee, and that there had been an offer for her, from "someone who wouldn't show her face. I don't take those." He would not settle anything by rock-paper-scissors, with either hand.

After the hour she put his cushion against the alcove post, with the chest of drawers behind it. Every morning he assembled the rifle on the veranda and took it apart again; there was nothing to shoot. In the fourth year he mended the gutter and would not take her hundred-yen coin: "When I take money, someone dies. I'd have to ask you who." From the eleventh year he called a hand for her Sunday games against the garden wall, but never put out his own. His hair went grey, the eyebrows last.

When Sazae, past seventy and losing her memory, offered him her purse as payment, he said, "It isn't enough," and did not touch it. It lay on the table for six years.

In the last winter she left the chest of drawers pulled away from the wall, and he saw it and stayed on his cushion. That Sunday she came back with the tea behind the post, and his arm went, with the edge of the hand. He had the tray in his other hand before she was down. He poured both cups, then picked up the purse and counted it: three thousand three hundred. The house went while he was closing the clasp.
