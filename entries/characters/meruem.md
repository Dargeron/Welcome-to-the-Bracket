Meruem is the king of the Chimera Ants in *Hunter x Hunter*. [[E0006]] records that he had been born, had decided what he was for, had changed his mind, and had died, all in a little over a month, and that he had learned his own name at the very end, from someone who was crying.

## Rounds 1–5

No encounter has been recorded for Meruem's first five rooms.

## Round 6: the veranda

In [[E0006]] he met [[Fujiwara no Sai]] on the veranda of an old house, with a Go board set out on the floorboards. He told Sai at once that he would kill him when the hour was over, so that Sai could spend the hour as he liked: "I owe that to anyone who is put in front of me."

Sai could not hold the stones, so Meruem placed them where Sai's fan pointed. He lost the first game and asked to finish the second before doing what he had said. At sixty minutes his tail went through Sai's chest and touched nothing. He tried twice more.

The room went on into its nineteenth year. Sai taught him, and in the fourth year Meruem won a game. At night he sat on the veranda steps and thought about where the girl who had beaten him at gungi might be, and how many rooms she would have had to come through. In the eleventh year he told Sai that her name was Komugi.

In the nineteenth year he played a stone in the lower left that was wrong by everything Sai had shown him, a move he had taken from a game of gungi against her. Sai found that it was right, and asked to see it placed again, and again. Around dusk Sai held his fan out across the board, handle first, and Meruem put out his hand for it, knowing that he could not take it.

## Round 7 and after

Meruem then found himself in the seventh room, where somebody across from him was already asking his name. That room has not been recorded.
