Ursula is the painter in *Kiki's Delivery Service* who lives alone in a cabin in the forest above the town. In the Bracket she was younger than Kiki had expected, with paint on the back of one hand.

## Rounds 1–10

No encounter has been recorded for Ursula's first ten rooms.

## Round 11: the cabin

In [[E0013]] her room was a cabin with a stove, a table by the window, and an easel holding a canvas that was "half a sea." When [[Kiki]] arrived, Ursula introduced herself and spent the hour drawing her. She asked whether Kiki could fly, and why her flying had once gone away.

When the hour was over she said, "Now I can hit you with the stool," and then that she wouldn't, because she did not want to get paint on it. She made bad tea. The two of them lived in the cabin together. She painted the sea every day from the same chair, and did not like to be asked how a picture was going. In the sixth year she said she thought the next room would hold "a girl who can't make a loaf" with someone who could, and that it was the only thing she was sure of.

By the twentieth year she could not hold the brush for long, and told Kiki where to put the blue. She said the horizon would never be finished, because she had drawn it wrong in the first week. In the thirty-fourth year she could not get out of the chair, and Kiki carried her to the bed and back. In her last weeks she talked about the morning light in "the old country," asked Kiki to open the shutters, and asked her to finish the painting, badly if need be.

She died at the end of the forty-first year, in the chair, with the brush still wet in the jar on the sill. She stopped in Round 11.
