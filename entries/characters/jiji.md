Jiji is the black cat in *Kiki's Delivery Service* who talks to his witch, Kiki, and whom, by the end of the film, she can no longer understand. He is a separate entrant from [[Kiki]]; in [[E0013]] she noticed on arriving in her eleventh room that her cat was not with her.

## Rounds 1–2

No encounter has been recorded for Jiji's first two rooms.

## Round 3: the kitchen

In [[E0016]] he arrived on the counter of a small kitchen with two bowls by the door, one marked CAT and one marked DOG. On the floor was [[Ein]]. Jiji had talked all his life to one person and found, now that someone else answered, that he did not know where to put his face. During the hour they traded accounts of their people; he said Kiki was thirteen when she left home and that he was "the one with the sense." When Ein made a joke, Jiji laughed.

Nothing happened when the hour ran out. Jiji came down and ate fish from the CAT bowl. He tried the window, which was painted shut. He thought about Kiki, who had stopped understanding him at a point he could not name, perhaps when she was fourteen; the kitchen was the first place in a year where anyone understood him. He said so on about the third day.

He taught Ein to keep very still, as he had once done in a shop window when a cat was needed to be a toy, and Ein showed him his trick of playing dead. They did not fight. Once, after an argument about the window, Jiji put out a claw and Ein put up his lip, and then both went to their bowls.

In Ein's last month Jiji brought him water in his mouth. On the last evening Ein played dead for him one more time. Jiji sat with his paws together, "being a good audience," and waited for the ear to twitch.

## Round 4 and after

He was still waiting when the room went. No later room has been recorded.
