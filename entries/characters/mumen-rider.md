Mumen Rider is a hero in *One-Punch Man* who has no special powers, rides a bicycle, and wears a helmet. In the Bracket he described himself as "very low on the list."

## Rounds 1–12

No encounter has been recorded for Mumen Rider's first twelve rooms.

## Round 13: the bus stop

In [[E0017]] he came round the corner on his bicycle and skidded to a stop at a bus stop where [[Saitama]] sat on the bench. "I thought I was late," he said. During the hour he asked Saitama how he had become strong and whether his hair had come back. He said he rode the bicycle everywhere, because he had no money for anything else and because it was the only thing he was good at, and then looked surprised at having said so. When Saitama said winning was boring, he did not offer to fix it.

After the hour he decided not to ride away. When Saitama said he could now hit him, Mumen Rider asked, "You won't, though?" Saitama said no, and he took off his helmet and said, "Then you'd better get used to me."

He counted Saitama's morning push-ups, riding slow circles round him and getting the count wrong. He spent a summer teaching Saitama to ride, holding the back of the saddle and telling him to look up. Over the years he rode less, then not at all, then rode a small bicycle made from the frame of the big one. In about the fortieth year he noticed Saitama move his push-ups to the afternoons and "took the change as a sign, and did not say so."

Saitama died on the bench in the fifty-first year. Mumen Rider sat with him until the light had gone off the road, then put on his helmet and stood the bicycle upright.

## Round 14 and after

The encounter ends with Mumen Rider on his bicycle on a slope he did not know, riding down it without looking back, towards a bend he wanted to see round. That room has not been recorded.
