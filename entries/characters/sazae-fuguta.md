Sazae Fuguta is the title character of *Sazae-san*, eldest daughter of the Isono house, wife of Masuo and mother of Tara-chan. She has been twenty-four every Sunday evening since 1969.

## Round 1

No encounter has been recorded for Sazae's first room.

## Round 2: the house with six cushions

In [[E0011]] she arrived in a house with a low table and six cushions, called through the rooms for Tara-chan, and found [[Golgo 13]] standing against the alcove post. She took him for a guest and made tea. When he told her not to walk behind him, because he hit anyone who did without deciding to, she brought the tea the long way round, through the corridor and in by the other door. He told her he shot people for a fee and that he had refused an offer for her; "Then you're only a guest," she said.

The hour ended unnoticed. Afterwards she moved the table so that his cushion was against the alcove post and pushed the chest of drawers into the gap behind it. When he mended the gutter he would not take her hundred-yen coin, so she gave him the larger piece of mackerel. On Sunday evenings she played rock-paper-scissors against the garden wall, and from the eleventh year he called a hand most weeks.

In that house she turned twenty-five, and in the ninth year found a grey hair. Past seventy she began to lose the ends of things, and called him Masuo-san, Father, and once Tama. On a clear morning she set her open purse before him, three thousand two hundred yen and the coin. "I'm going to forget about your back," she said. "So this is work." He did not touch it. It stayed on the table for six years.

In the last winter she stopped calling out "Sweeping" when she swept, and one day pulled the chest of drawers away from the wall to look for the cat and did not push it back. That Sunday he called "Rock," and she went in to make the tea and came back behind the post. His arm went, "the edge of the hand," as he had explained in the first hour. She stopped in Round 2.
