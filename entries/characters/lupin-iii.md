Lupin III is the gentleman thief of *Lupin the Third*, grandson of Arsène Lupin, pursued for decades by [[Inspector Zenigata]] of the ICPO.

## Rounds 1–8

No encounter has been recorded for Lupin's first eight rooms.

## Round 9: the station

In [[E0007]] he arrived in a police station with one desk and one cell, and Zenigata had him in handcuffs before either had said hello. During the hour the cuffs stayed shut. Zenigata read the charges from memory, by year, and Lupin answered each one, usually "Guilty," sometimes adding that it had been Jigen, or Fujiko. When the inspector reached impersonation of a police officer on eleven occasions, Lupin said, "Fourteen."

When the hour ended he was out of the cuffs at once, handed them back, and let Zenigata put them on him again. When Zenigata later found the magazine and the chambered round missing from his pistol and told him to give it back, Lupin asked, "Give what back?" The encounter records at its end that a magazine had been taped under the desk, a hand's width from the inspector's knee, for thirty-eight years.

Each night, once Zenigata was asleep at the desk, Lupin let himself out, walked to the mailbox up the street and back, put the inspector's coat over him, and locked himself into the cell again before morning. In the evenings he told Zenigata how old cases had been done, "and most of it was true." When the inspector's hands grew too stiff for the cuffs, Lupin did them up himself.

Over four years of nights he forged the signature of the Secretary General of the ICPO, with a seal cut from Zenigata's eraser, in the RECEIVED BY box of the inspector's arrest report. In the thirty-eighth winter he brought the report to the dying Zenigata, who called it forged, and Lupin agreed. After Zenigata died, Lupin laid the cuffs on the report, returned the magazine to the pistol and the pistol to its holster, and went back into the cell. He was feeling for the lock when the station went.

## Round 10 and after

No later room has been recorded.
