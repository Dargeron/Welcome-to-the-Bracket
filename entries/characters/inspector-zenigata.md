Inspector Koichi Zenigata is the ICPO officer of *Lupin the Third* who has spent his career trying to arrest [[Lupin III]].

## Rounds 1–8

No encounter has been recorded for Zenigata's first eight rooms. In [[E0007]] he said he had come through eight and had asked in every one of them whether anyone had seen Lupin.

## Round 9: the station

In [[E0007]] he met Lupin in a police station with one desk and one cell, and had him in handcuffs before either had said hello. He spent the hour reading the charges from memory, by year, and writing each answer in the box provided. After the hour he put the cuffs back on and told Lupin he was "in custody pending transfer."

While reading the charges he had worked out that a prisoner is delivered alive, and that an officer neither kills a man in his custody nor lets him die of anything he could prevent. That left one way for the room to finish. He took his pistol into the back, where the kettle was. It clicked: the magazine and the chambered round were gone. He made two cups of instant noodles and took one to the cell.

He searched the cell every week for thirty-eight years and never found the magazine. He found his own hat twice, his badge once, and a drawing of himself asleep at the desk. He wrote the arrest report in the first month and again, more fairly, in the third year, and kept it in the top drawer; every morning he took it out and looked down the street.

In the thirty-eighth winter he could not get up from the cot behind the desk, and asked for the top drawer. Lupin brought him the report with the RECEIVED BY box filled in with a forged signature and seal. "This is forged," he said. Then he set his own seal beside it, pressing with the heel of his hand, and said "Prisoner transferred" toward the door. He asked for his hat, and Lupin put it on him. He died in the night, with the noodles half eaten.
