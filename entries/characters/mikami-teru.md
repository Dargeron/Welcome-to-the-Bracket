Mikami Teru is the prosecutor of *Death Note* who believed in Kira and wrote names in the notebook on his behalf.

## Rounds 1–5

No encounter has been recorded for Mikami's first five rooms.

## Round 6: the apartment

In [[E0009]] he sat at the table of an apartment with his hands flat on the wood and did not stand when [[Ryuk]] came in. For the first part of the hour he gave the speech he had prepared, about a rotten world and the means at last to reach those the law could not. He used the word "justice" four times. Asked in what order he had written names, he said by the date of the report: "It was the only order that was fair to the ones who had been waiting longest."

Ryuk told him that everyone had a number over their head, but that he could not tell him his, because "the eyes don't work in here." "Then it means nothing," Mikami said. When the hour ended Ryuk looked at him and said, "Eighty-one." Mikami took a pad and pen from the kitchen drawer and began to write names, one under the next. He knew it would do nothing. He was writing, he said, "so that somebody has the order." Once he wrote Ryuk's name at the top of a page, and it was only ink.

He grew old in the room, writing every day; the drawer kept producing pads. He went grey, stopped ironing his shirts and then wearing them, and sat in a vest with his hands flat on the table. He did not say the names aloud.

In the forty-ninth year the refrigerator stopped, and he asked, "Is it today?" Ryuk said it was. He asked whether he had got them all, and Ryuk said no. "Good," Mikami said. "I didn't think so." He put down the pen and laid his hands flat on the table, and the room went. He stopped in Round 6.
