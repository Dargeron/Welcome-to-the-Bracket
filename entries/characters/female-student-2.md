Female Student 2 was a background character with a single appearance in her source: in episode eleven, in a hallway, Female Student 1 asks her about the transfer student and she nods. She shared her face with the girl who sat behind her, and her hair was drawn as one piece.

## Round 1

In [[E0001|her only room]], a classroom, she was paired with [[Light Yagami]]. She had planned to nod through the hour, but the Address required her to talk, and she found she could. When he asked her name she discovered that she did not have one; she knew only that she was listed as Female Student 2. The writing inside her indoor shoes and in her notebook was squiggles.

The two of them spent the hour searching for her name. Her best lead was an unanswered roll call in episode four, fourth from the end, a name ending in "ko." He recited candidate names while she watched for recognition; "Tomoko" felt close once and then did not. She understood, before the hour was over, that he needed her full name in order to kill her, and she helped anyway.

When the hour ended without a name, he strangled her with his tie. Her hands resisted although she had decided not to. She stopped in Round 1.

## Afterwards

The archive's own coda to [[E0001]] records that someone went looking for the rest of her name afterwards and found nothing behind "ko." No name was ever decided for her.
