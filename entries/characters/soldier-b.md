## Round 1

In [[E0002]] he was paired with the unlisted concert-goer he would come to call [[Orange]]. When the hour ended he raised his rifle because that seemed to be what it was for, and could not make it fire. By the time they found the safety, neither of them wanted to pull the trigger.

They stayed on the hillside for about fifty years. They learned to keep a fire in during the first year, gave each other names, and shared one blanket from roughly the fourth year on. He kept the name B and liked it. When Orange could not remember her mother's face, he gave her one, a mother with a mole by the eye who oversalted everything, and she in turn gave him a town with a river, a bridge past the school and a sergeant who couldn't whistle.

In old age he urged her to go on without him: to win, and to ask for him too. He died of old age in the autumn, complaining about the beans, with Orange holding his hand. He stopped in Round 1, and the hill went with him.
