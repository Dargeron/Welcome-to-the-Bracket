Shinji Ikari is the fourteen-year-old pilot of *Neon Genesis Evangelion*. In [[E0010]] he arrived with his music player, which went from track twenty-five to track twenty-six and back, and never ran down.

## Round 1: the house with the stairs on the wrong side

In [[E0010]] he was at the kitchen table when [[Misae Nohara]] arrived. He stood up, said he was sorry, and sat down again. He told her that his mother was dead and that his father wanted him to fight things from the sea in a robot, and he did not know whether he was paid. He told her what little he remembered of his mother: a white coat, and a voice saying his name from above him. At about forty minutes he told her he had killed the last person who had been nice to him, and that the person had asked him to.

When the hour ran out Misae ground her knuckles into his temples, and it hurt. He made a noise that started as a laugh. Then she gave him the daikon to peel.

He lived with her in the house until he was sixty-six. He cooked better than she did. His voice broke at sixteen; at nineteen he was taller than her and let her mark it on the doorframe. He did the heavy digging. He told her about the cello. Around thirty he stopped talking for a whole winter and stood each day at the edge of the garden, where the room stopped; in the spring he came in and ate the food she had left on the step. He never called her anything.

When she was dying, at eighty-one, she asked him, if he got Himawari, to raise her and take his time about the rest: "Not before she can light the stove." He said he heard her. He was sitting with her when she died.

## Round 2 and after

He was fourteen again, with the wires in his ears and track twenty-five starting. The encounter's last line records that the first thing he did in the second room was look down at the floor. That room has not been recorded.
