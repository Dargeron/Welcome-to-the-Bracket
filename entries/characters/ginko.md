Ginko is the wandering mushi-master of *Mushishi*. In [[E0008]] he had white hair, one green eye, and a wooden pack the size of a cupboard with a great many small drawers.

## Rounds 1–5

No encounter has been recorded for Ginko's first five rooms.

## Round 6: the house in the field

In [[E0008]] he was at the table of a small house in a field when [[Kino]] arrived. He asked where her motorrad was before she had mentioned one. He told her his own rule: he did not sleep under the same roof more than a week, because the mushi followed him and "a house gets ideas." In the room nothing came down from the ceiling. "Nothing here at all," he said. "I haven't been this quiet since I was a boy."

After the hour, when Kino put a hand on her pistol, he said, "I'd rather you didn't, but I won't take it personally," and went on peeling a root.

On the third evening she walked to the hedge and came back and knocked. He opened the door and said, "Traveller. Welcome. How long do you plan to stay?" He did so every third evening after that, and got better at it. He began asking about her papers and set a fee for entry of one story. When she repeated a story he told her she had already used it, and gave her change.

In the ninth year he found that the tin of tea was only drawn as tea and refilled itself, and laughed until he coughed. In the fourteenth year he got a cough that did not go away. By the nineteenth his hair had gone "the white of a plate" and he could not hold a cigarette; Kino lit one and he let it burn down in his hand. He told her he had broken his rule in the first week and slept under that roof every night.

In the thirty-first year Kino came back from the hedge, knocked, and nobody came. She found him in his chair, with the pack beside it and the stove going. He stopped in Round 6.
