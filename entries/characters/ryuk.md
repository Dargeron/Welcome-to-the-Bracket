Ryuk is the shinigami of *Death Note* who dropped his notebook into the human world. In [[E0009]] he was taller than the door frame, with a grin that went most of the way across his face, and he arrived with an apple in his hand.

## Rounds 1–5

No encounter has been recorded for Ryuk's first five rooms.

## Round 6: the apartment

In [[E0009]] he met [[Mikami Teru]] in an apartment with one table, two chairs, a refrigerator and a window onto a brick wall. "Nice place," he said. "Who pays the rent?" He listened to Mikami's account of justice without reacting, asked in what order he had written the names, and laughed at the answer: "I'd have written them all at once and then sat around being bored."

He told Mikami that everyone had a number over their head, which he could normally see, but that "the eyes don't work in here," so anything he said would be only his word. When the hour ended he stood and looked at Mikami's head for a long time, and said, "Eighty-one." Then he offered him the rest of his apple core.

The room went on into its forty-ninth year. A bowl of apples appeared in the cupboard; Ryuk did not like them, refilled the bowl each morning, and ate none. He watched Mikami write names on pad after pad. When Mikami asked what good he was, Ryuk said, "I'm good for watching." Asked once, years in, what the number was now, he said it was still eighty-one.

In the forty-ninth year the refrigerator stopped. "It's today," Ryuk told him. Mikami asked whether he had got them all, and Ryuk said, "No."

## Afterwards

When the room went, Ryuk was standing in a field at the edge of a row of other drawn people looking around, with one apple that he had kept. He finished it; the encounter says it was the last one. What that field is, and his next room, have not been recorded.
