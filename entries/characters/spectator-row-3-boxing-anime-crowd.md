Spectator, Row 3 was a face in the crowd at a title fight in an unnamed boxing anime. He was on screen for a second and a half during a pan across the seats in the fourth round, with his right fist raised. He had four drawings: fist up, fist higher, mouth open, mouth shut. The same drawing, flipped, was used for [[Spectator, Row 7 (boxing anime crowd)]].

## Round 1: the boxing hall

In [[E0015]] he stood up in an empty boxing hall and met Row 7 across the ring. They had the same jacket, jaw and three lines of hair, partings on opposite sides, and a single shared voice. He had been shouting for the champion; Row 7 had been shouting for the boy in the blue corner. Neither knew how the fight had ended, and they argued about it for the hour.

After the hour they tried to box. Row 3 threw his right as Row 7 threw his left, and the fists met in the middle, every time, for three days. They stopped on the fourth. They lived on fried noodles and beer from a counter under the stands, and Row 3 slept in the red dressing room.

In the evenings they finished the fight by telling it, each allowed to refuse what the other said happened. The fight stayed in its fifth round for nine years and its sixth for longer. Row 3 named the girl in the second row Noriko, and after that she was always there, in a yellow coat. The two men went bald in the same month. By then Row 3 was calling most of the kid's punches.

They fell ill in the same winter, and Row 3 was worse by an afternoon. At ringside he told Row 7 to finish the fight: "Say it. I'll say yes." Row 7 told the knockdown and began the count. At seven, Row 3 had his right fist in the air. After nine there was no hall. He stopped in Round 1.
