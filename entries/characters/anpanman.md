Anpanman is a hero whose head is a bread bun baked by Uncle Jam. He feeds the hungry by giving them pieces of it. He is known in the archive from a single room, which the founding text tells in his own vocabulary, about four hundred words long.

## Round 1

In [[E0005]] he woke in a meadow with an apple tree, across from a man who introduced himself as [[Johan Liebert|Johan]]. He took the Address calmly, since stories are nice. During the hour Johan asked him who he was, and the only answer he found was "I am Anpanman." Neither of them wanted to fight when the hour ended.

He brought Johan an apple, then berries, which Johan set in the grass uneaten. On the fifth day Johan was too weak to stand and said he was very hungry. Anpanman understood why the food was being left there, and that Uncle Jam was in another meadow, far away, with somebody else. He fed Johan from his own face anyway, a piece a day, until he could not fly, then could not see, and finally had no face at all.

He sat down in the shade to wait for Uncle Jam, and Johan waited with him. He stopped in Round 1.
