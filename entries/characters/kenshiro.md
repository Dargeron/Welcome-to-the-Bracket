Kenshiro is the hero of *Fist of the North Star*, a martial artist in a ruined world who kills by striking the points of the body, and who bears seven scars on his chest in the pattern of the Big Dipper. In his source he searches for Yuria, the woman taken from him.

## Round 1

No encounter has been recorded for Kenshiro's first room.

## Round 2: the valley

In [[E0014]] he was sitting on the edge of a well in a dry valley, with a stone hut nearby, when the [[Zeed Gang Member (Fist of the North Star, episode 1)]] arrived. The gang member recognised him: Kenshiro had killed him in episode one of their source. Kenshiro thought about it honestly and said he did not remember.

He was not a talker, and the hour was hard on him; the gang member did most of it. Kenshiro told him, in few words, that he had to go on, because he was looking for a woman, and said her name, Yuria. The gang member asked whether his death could take longer this time.

When the hour was over Kenshiro put two fingers into him, lower than the first time and a little to the left, and said, "Forty years. To the day." The two of them lived in the valley for those forty years. Kenshiro mended the hut and spoke perhaps a hundred words in a good year. About once a year he said Yuria's name, not to anyone. In the year the gang member could not stop counting, Kenshiro answered him the same way every time: "To the day."

On the last day the two of them sat on either side of the well through the morning. The gang member died around midday. Kenshiro, "who had been counting too and had never said so," went on looking at the squash until the valley went.
