Spectator, Row 7 was a face in the crowd at a title fight in an unnamed boxing anime: the drawing of [[Spectator, Row 3 (boxing anime crowd)]], flipped left to right and reused in the seventh row on the far side of the ring. Being flipped, he faced the other way, raised his left fist instead of his right, and his breast pocket was not over his heart.

## Round 1: the boxing hall

In [[E0015]] he met Row 3 across the ring of an empty boxing hall. They shared one voice. Facing the other way, he had spent his second and a half on screen looking at the blue corner, "at the boy with the swollen eye," and had put his fist up for him. Row 3 had been shouting for the champion. They argued about the fight for the hour, neither knowing how it had ended.

When the hour was up he suggested they "do it the way they did." They climbed into the ring, but their punches met in the middle every time, and after three days they stopped. He slept in the blue dressing room.

In the evenings they went on with the fight by telling it, each able to refuse the other's account. It was Row 7 who mentioned that the champion was thirty-four and that there was a girl of about ten in the second row whom the champion took care not to look at. Over the years he came to call most of the champion's punches.

When they both fell ill, he helped Row 3 to his seat at ringside. Asked to finish the fight, he said the champion feinted low, came over the top with the right, and put the kid down by the blue corner. Nothing came back across the ring. He counted aloud. At seven Row 3 had his fist raised. Row 7 said nine, stopped, and waited to be told. Then there was no hall.
