The Frog Worker was one of the frog-men who serve at the bathhouse in *Spirited Away*. In the film he was among those who ran to No-Face's gold and was swallowed. He spoke with a lisp and arrived in the Bracket in his work clothes "with his hands already out, because they usually were."

## Round 1

No encounter has been recorded for his first room.

## Round 2: the dock

In [[E0012]] he met [[No-Face]] on a dock beside flat grey water. He had met "this customer" before; it had gone badly, "from the inside." During the hour he stood at an angle so that he could run in more than one direction. When No-Face produced a lump of gold, he bit it out of professional habit and kept it. He did most of the talking: about the bathhouse, the Witch who ran it, and the day a girl from the human side cleaned the Stink Spirit, which he called the best day of his working life and said he had mostly missed.

After the hour No-Face gave him a second, larger piece of gold, and he said "Thank you." There was nothing on the dock to spend it on. He built a pile, and came to understand that the gold was for the thank you. He taught No-Face *because* and *please*. In the third year No-Face began speaking in the Frog's own voice, and the Frog, who had been told never to argue with a customer who was a spirit, let him keep it.

He lived fifty-three years in the room, walking slower each year. On the last morning he lay on a folded coat beside the pile, which came up to his hip. "I was going to say it was a good pile," he said, and No-Face said it for him, in his voice. He stopped in Round 2.
