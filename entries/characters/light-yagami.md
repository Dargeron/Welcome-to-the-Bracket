Light Yagami is never named in the text of his only recorded encounter, where he is "the boy" with the window seat, second from the back. The archive identifies him by his method: he had killed several thousand people without being in the room for any of them, and he did it in writing, needing only a face and a name.

## Round 1

In [[E0001]] he was paired with [[Female Student 2]] in a classroom. He had her face already and had allowed forty seconds for the rest. He spent the hour, politely and with professional interest, trying to recover her name: her indoor shoes, her notebook, an unanswered roll call in episode four, and a long list of names ending in "ko."

None of it worked, because nobody had ever decided what she was called. At minute fifty-eight he dropped the nice voice and called her furniture. After the hour he checked his black notebook one last time, put it away, and killed her by hand with his tie, a method the encounter describes as new to him.

He survived Round 1. No later room of his has been recorded.
