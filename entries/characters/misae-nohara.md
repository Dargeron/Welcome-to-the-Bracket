Misae Nohara is the mother of the Nohara family in *Crayon Shin-chan*: wife of Hiroshi, mother of Shinnosuke and the baby Himawari, and owner of the dog Shiro. In [[E0010]] she was twenty-nine, "and meant to go on being it."

## Round 1: the house with the stairs on the wrong side

In [[E0010|her only room]] she met [[Shinji Ikari]] in a house whose stairs were on the wrong side. She knew at once that she was not going to kill a child. She asked where his mother was, and was appalled that he did not know whether he was paid. During the hour she talked about her family: Hiroshi would be all right; Shinnosuke "would either be the first one in the whole Bracket to stop or he would win it." When she reached Himawari she stopped, and went back to the dog. When Shinji told her he had killed someone who had asked him to, she said, "Well, I'm not asking," and went to look in the cupboards.

When the hour ran out she ground her knuckles into his temples, as she did at home. It hurt, and she saw him understand what that meant. Then she gave him the daikon to peel.

She lived in the house until she was eighty-one. She kept the hens; he cooked, better than she did, which put her out for most of a year. She hid his music player in the rice tin and the coop, and settled for sharing an earpiece at dinner. She marked his height against the doorframe when he was nineteen. At sixty or so she told him that she had given her life to someone else's son while her own were out there doing God knows what, and he said yes. She called him Shinji-kun throughout.

At eighty-one she got a cough. She gave him instructions about the hens, and then about Himawari: if he got her, he was to raise her and take his time about the rest of it. "Not before she can light the stove." She also told him to stop saying sorry to people. She died two days later, in the morning, with Shinji sitting beside her.
