Frieren is an elf mage with white hair in two tails and the face of a fifteen-year-old, who gave her age as something over a thousand years. She is one of **the Sixteen**: she survives to the end of the Timeline and cannot stop in any encounter.

## Rounds 1–2

No encounter has been recorded for Frieren's first two rooms. In [[E0004]] she refers to "an earlier room" in which she waited until afterwards to say something, and found there was no afterwards.

## Round 3: the road

In [[E0004]] she was reading on a milestone beside a forest road when [[Orange]] arrived. She asked about the hill: what had been grown there and what [[Soldier B]] had liked to eat. She slept in the middle of the road, and her protection stopped Orange's stone three times without waking her. In the morning she said she could lose, but wouldn't.

She walked with Orange for fifty-one years. She studied the orange light for eleven of them and in the twelfth made one of her own, the same colour, with no switch. She collected Orange's borrowed past a piece at a time. When Orange was dying she told her that she had liked the walking.

## Round 4 and after

After Orange's death Frieren appeared on a beach, across from somebody new, asking what they liked to eat. That room has not been recorded. The archive notes that she now asks the question in every room.
